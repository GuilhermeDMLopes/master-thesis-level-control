# MPC V4 target-17000 real-plant validation

## Result

The final high-range MPC V4 validation completed the full 180 s active window
on 2026-08-29. The measured physical level reached 15 cm and stabilized. The
controller tracked `SP_RAW=17000` without violating the actuator, level, rate
or watchdog limits, and the independent supervisor verified zero output after
terminating FORTE.

This run is the final real-plant MPC performance evidence. The earlier V3H and
first V4 runs remain preserved as commissioning and target-refinement history.

## Final configuration

- application: `MPC_REAL_RAW_SAFE_V4`;
- resource: `FORTE_PC.ResRealRawMPCV4`;
- controller: `MPC_MOVE_BLOCKED_NMPC_V4`;
- controller cycle: 500 ms;
- target: 17000 raw, physically observed as 15 cm;
- DAC envelope: 0..16000;
- maximum controller move: 750 DAC per 500 ms;
- supervised active duration: 180 s;
- supervisor limits: 19000 median raw, 20000 instantaneous raw and 500 raw/s;
- physical operator abort: 20 cm.

The gateway's legacy `level_cm=raw/1000` field is not used as the final
physical calibration. The authoritative physical result is the manual 15 cm
observation paired with the raw-count trajectory.

## Interpretation

The response shows the expected constrained behavior. The controller initially
uses the 16000 DAC ceiling to fill the tank, then progressively reduces the
command as the level approaches the target. During the final interval it moves
between 13500 and 14500 DAC to balance the continuous bottom drain. The final
mean remains within 1% of the raw target, while the maximum median overshoot is
approximately 0.5%.

The first attempt with the refined target was interrupted at 36.9 s because
the PLC watchdog tripped after a long gateway/PLC disconnection earlier in the
day. That attempt did not violate an MPC level, rate or DAC limit. The gateway
rearmed safely, FORTE was terminated and zero output was verified. A fresh
gateway and FORTE stack then remained healthy for a 90 s protected deployment
check and throughout the complete 180 s final run.

## Safety evidence

- 695 protected predeployment samples remained at zero output;
- the watchdog remained healthy throughout all 1481 active samples;
- the run ended with `ACTIVE_WINDOW_COMPLETE`;
- FORTE was terminated by the independent supervisor;
- gateway and PLC zero commands were sent;
- 74 independent post-shutdown samples confirmed zero output;
- FORTE and gateway were stopped and ports 61499/4841 were closed.

The successful run does not authorize unattended or unrestricted future real
actuation. It closes the bounded experimental validation required for the
dissertation.
